Work / Broward-Miami Health Institute

  • Client
  • Website
  • SEO
  • GoHighLevel
  • AI chatbot

254 leads — and 49% of them came from Google.

An AHA-authorized healthcare school in Miramar, Florida needed students to find it on Google and register online. I built the whole system in GoHighLevel — the 24-step website and lead funnel, the SEO and speed work, the CRM and the follow-up automations — so every search turns into a registered, nurtured lead. Enrollment and payment happen on campus; my job was the leads.

01

The problem

The site was gaining visibility but losing clicks, and it was slow where it mattered most — on phones.

32mobile PageSpeed score (Sep 30 baseline)
15.2smobile load time (LCP)
~8 MBloaded before the homepage was usable
  • Five competing versions of the site in Google (www, non-www, courses., old paths, portal) splitting ranking signals.
  • Checkout and registration pages indexed when they shouldn't be.
  • Broken schema (placeholder logo, stale dates, fake ratings) and conflicting course facts across pages.
  • A 4.9 MB hero video, a 1.5 MB logo and 1.7 MB of chat and form scripts loading up front.
02

What I built

A 24-step website & lead funnel

Home, a courses hub, 15 program pages, registration, enrollment, checkout and policy pages — a guided path: Browse Courses → Register → Enroll → Pay & Start.

SEO & speed (from Sep 30)

  • Consolidated everything onto one HTTPS domain with one-hop redirects and self canonicals; checkout pages set to noindex.
  • Rebuilt the schema as EducationalOrganization + LocalBusiness — 2 valid Rich Results, 0 errors / 0 warnings.
  • New titles and descriptions for 8 priority program pages; fixed contradictory course facts.
  • Hero video 4.9 MB → 1.26 MB, logo to WebP, chat loader delayed until interaction.

CRM & AI follow-up

  • Registration Confirmation + Nurture — a separate email + SMS sequence per program (≈15 branches), each stopping the moment a lead moves forward.
  • AI admissions chatbot on GoHighLevel Conversation AI, trained on a custom knowledge base.
  • Appointment alerts, a 7-stage pipeline, a Program Interest field with 16 tags and first-touch lead attribution.
