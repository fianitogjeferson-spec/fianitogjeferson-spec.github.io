Work / JP Health Care Services

  • Client
  • SEO

  • Security
  • Local pages

Recovered a site from a 17,000-account spam takeover.

A licensed home care agency with offices in Miramar and Miami Gardens was buried under bot accounts and spam pages, loaded slowly on phones and had no pages for the two cities it serves. In about four weeks I removed the spam from Google, stopped it at the source, fixed the technical SEO, sped up key pages and built three local landing pages.

01

The problem

17,740user accounts — almost all bots, +250 a day
~5,800spam profile pages indexed by Google
27Contact page mobile score · main content at 19s
  • Open course sign-ups created a public profile page for every bot — and Google indexed them.
  • No SEO plugin: 0 of 33 pages had a meta description, 13 had no H1, 19 images had no alt text.
  • No page targeting "home care Miramar" or "Miami Gardens", although both offices exist.
  • 4.1 MB of uncompressed images and a 2.8 MB booking embed on the Contact page.
02

What I did

Stopped the spam

  • Made every spam profile return 410 Gone and requested removal in Search Console.
  • Deleted spam in reviewed batches (17,740 → 1,988), protecting every real and admin account.
  • Added email verification and reCAPTCHA, then found and closed the gap bots used to skip it.

Fixed the foundations

  • Rank Math setup, a clean sitemap, noindex on 22 internal pages, broken links and old URLs redirected.
  • New titles and descriptions on 9 pages, one H1 per page, alt text on every image, 40 form fields labelled.
  • Local business schema for both offices — 3 valid Rich Results.

Built local relevance

  • New pages: Home Care in Miramar, Home Care in Miami Gardens, Live Scan Fingerprinting.
  • Services page rebuilt, FAQ 5 → 11 questions, real Google reviews on the homepage and a review request kit.

Ran it safely

Backups before every change, staging tests, a 68-entry change log with rollback, and daily, weekly and monthly reports in plain language.
