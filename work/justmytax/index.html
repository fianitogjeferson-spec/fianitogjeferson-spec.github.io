A launch-ready funnel that never pitches an enrolled student.

JustMyTax needed a complete sales system for "The Ultimate Tax Agent", a 5-week live course. I designed and wrote the funnel, connected Stripe and built three automations — each checking live enrollment status before every message. The system is fully built and tested; the client paused the project before launch.

What I built

A 6-step funnel

A sales page with offer positioning and an objection-handling FAQ, a 2-step Stripe checkout, an access page and policy pages — on a custom "tax form" visual identity.

Three automations, email + SMS

  • Lead nurture from a "Filing Status Cheat Sheet" lead magnet — teaching emails, a student story and SMS nudges, with 7 "already enrolled?" checks.
  • Abandoned-checkout recovery at 1 h, 24 h and 60 h, re-checking enrollment before each touch.
  • Onboarding for every new student.
