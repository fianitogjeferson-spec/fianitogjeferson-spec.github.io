A new Swiss website with 23 searches on page 1.

JDS Pro Chalet renovates and maintains wooden chalets across Valais. It needed a site that local homeowners could actually find. I built it from scratch in Systeme.io, in French, with WhatsApp and phone buttons, a 24-hour quote promise, on-page SEO and a full Google Business Profile.

  • IndustryChalet renovation · Conthey, Valais (CH)
  • PlatformSysteme.io

01

What I built

  • A French-language website with service pages for every trade — painting, façade lasure, sanding, sandblasting, roofing, Velux, shutters and decks.
  • WhatsApp and phone CTAs backed by a "free quote within 24 hours" promise.
  • On-page SEO targeting village searches across Valais.
  • A Google Business Profile with a full description, hours, service area and ≈14 detailed services.
